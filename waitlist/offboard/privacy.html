Who we are

OFFBOARD is an early-stage product by VORNEAUX. This page explains what information we collect from visitors and waitlist members of OFFBOARD, why we collect it, and what choices you have. VORNEAUX is the data controller responsible for this information.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers only the OFFBOARD waitlist page you're on right now. If you've interacted with other VORNEAUX products, such as CADENCE, their own data practices are described in that product's own privacy policy.

Information we collect

When you join the OFFBOARD waitlist, we collect:

  • Your email address, so we can contact you when early access opens.
  • Your language preference, so we can write to you in the right language.
  • If you choose to answer them: how many subscriptions you'd want to cancel, and what you'd be willing to pay to never have to do it yourself. These two questions are optional and help us build something people actually need.
  • A small amount of technical information attached to your signup, such as the campaign or link that brought you here, and the date and time you joined.
  • A unique subscriber identifier, used only to recognize your entry and to power the unsubscribe link in our emails.

We never ask for your bank login or any other financial account access at this stage — that's a deliberate design choice, not just a policy — and we don't use cookies to track you across other websites.

How we use your information

We use what you share to:

  • Let you know when early access to OFFBOARD opens, and share meaningful updates about the product before then.
  • Understand whether the problem we're trying to solve is real, and for whom — this is the entire point of this page. Your optional answers directly shape whether and how we build OFFBOARD.
  • Keep basic records of who signed up and when, for our own internal tracking.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to show you ads.

Legal basis for processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, we rely on:

  • Your consent, for the optional questions (subscriptions to cancel, willingness to pay) — you can decline to answer either of them and still join the waitlist.
  • Our legitimate interest, for your email address and the basic technical data attached to it, in order to run a functioning waitlist and validate a product idea before building it.

You can withdraw consent or object at any time by unsubscribing or writing to us — see "Your rights" below.

Who we share it with

We keep the number of parties who see your information to a minimum:

  • Google Workspace, which we use to send emails and to store waitlist entries securely. Google processes this information on our behalf, under its own data processing terms.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, and we do not sell it under any circumstance.

International transfers

Because we use Google Workspace, your information may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where that happens, it's covered by Google's standard contractual clauses and other safeguards recognized under applicable data protection law.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist entry for as long as OFFBOARD is in development and you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we remove you from the active waitlist immediately, and keep a minimal record — your email, language, and signup/unsubscribe dates — for a limited period, only to keep our records accurate and to make sure we don't contact you again by mistake.

Cookies and local storage

This page doesn't use tracking cookies. It stores two small pieces of information directly in your browser:

  • Your language choice, so the page remembers it on your next visit.
  • Your subscriber identifier, so the page can recognize you if you come back to unsubscribe.

If we ever activate analytics tools such as Google Analytics on this page, we'll update this section beforehand to explain what's collected and give you a way to opt out.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you.
  • Correct it, if it's inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it.
  • Receive a copy of it in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at the address below — we'll respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Children's privacy

OFFBOARD is intended for adults managing their own subscriptions, and this page is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including relying on Google Workspace's security infrastructure for storage and email delivery. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information protected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as OFFBOARD evolves — for example, when we add a new question to the waitlist form, or move to a different backend. We'll update the date at the top of this page whenever we do, and for material changes, we'll let you know by email.

Contact us

For anything related to this policy or your information, write to us at contact@vorneaux.com. We're a small team building this in the open, and we'll get back to you personally.
