Before you go

Questions people ask first.

Is this just another budgeting app?

+

No. Budgeting apps ask you to manage a system. Cadence is built to hand you one decision — what's safe to spend today — calculated for you, with no categories, no envelopes, no weekly review ritual required.

Is my financial data safe?

+

Bank connections, when available, go through the same read-only, encrypted providers used across the industry — Cadence never sees your credentials and can't move money. Full details will be published before launch.

Do I have to connect my bank account?

+

No. Manual entry is a first-class option from day one, not an afterthought — useful if you're not ready to connect an account, or your bank isn't supported yet.

What if my country isn't supported at launch?

+

Cadence launches with a small number of currencies and tax regimes done properly, then expands. Tell us where you're based when you join — it directly shapes what we build next.

When does it launch, and what does early access mean?

+

There's no fixed date yet — this page exists to find out if enough people want this before we build it. Early access means you'll be invited to try it, and to influence it, before anyone else.
