TRUST

Your purchases. Your information.

What's storedThe receipt itself, and the details extracted from it — product, price, retailer, dates. That's what builds your record.
What's notReceipts aren't sold or shared with advertisers, and aren't used to build a marketing profile.
Why we need itEach field exists to answer one question: is a return, warranty or price change still relevant right now.
DeletionYou can delete a receipt or your account at any time; the underlying data is removed with it.
Early stageOwnership OS is pre-launch. Security practices will be documented in full before public release.
